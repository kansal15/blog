Felicitated as a Mentor at NIXI's IGICBS One-Year Celebration

  • Post by Gaurav Kansal
  • January 22, 2026

On 19 January 2026, NIXI celebrated one year of its Internet Governance Internship & Capacity Building Scheme (IGICBS) in New Delhi. I was felicitated at the event as a mentor for the internship programme.

What Is IGICBS?

IGICBS is a scheme run by the National Internet Exchange of India (NIXI). It helps young people in India learn about Internet governance. It brings together students, universities and policymakers. Experts guide the interns as mentors. The aim is to build skills and leadership, and to help India take a bigger part in global Internet governance.

The One-Year Event

  • The chief guest was Shri S. Krishnan, Secretary, MeitY.
  • Shri Bhuvnesh Kumar, CEO, UIDAI, Shri Sushil Pal, Joint Secretary, MeitY, and Dr Devesh Tyagi, CEO, NIXI, were also present.
  • The IGICBS portal was launched, and the IGICBS Impact Report was released.
  • In its first year, NIXI engaged and trained more than 10,000 participants.

My Role as a Mentor

As a mentor, I helped interns learn various technologies, including DNS, BGP and RPKI.

Thank You

Thanks to NIXI for the recognition and for running this programme. Thanks also to the interns for their interest and hard work.
